WHO WE ARE

Community is at
the heart of our work.

S.P.A.R.E A LIFE Inc. is based in the Bronx. Our work centers on preventing violence and promoting mental and physical health, helping build a safer, healthier community together.

Through opportunities to connect and support one another, we bring that commitment to life.
